A car is a motorized vehicle designed primarily for the transportation of passengers, typically seating between one to eight people. It consists of a metal or composite body structure mounted on a set of wheels, usually four, and is powered by an internal combustion engine, an electric motor, or a hybrid system that combines both. The car's main components include the engine, transmission, drivetrain, suspension, and braking system, all of which work together to provide motion and control.

Cars come in a variety of body styles and sizes, such as sedans, SUVs, hatchbacks, coupes, convertibles, and pickup trucks, each serving different needs and preferences. Modern vehicles are equipped with advanced technology including GPS navigation, entertainment systems, safety features like airbags and anti-lock brakes, and increasingly, autonomous driving capabilities.
